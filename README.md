I'm a Year 2 Computer Engineering student at KNUST (Kumasi, Ghana), graduating in 2028. I split my time between embedded systems and IoT builds on ESP32, and web development with React, Tailwind CSS, and Three.js. I also take on freelance web projects for real clients, including BS Studios and Matty Multimedia.

## What I Build

On the hardware side, I design multi-sensor ESP32 systems for real-world monitoring, from farm environments to water quality, complete with WiFi-hosted dashboards. On the web side, I build interactive, visually rich interfaces and 3D experiences, along with client sites focused on clean design and performance.

## Featured Projects

- **EduViz / e-LAB** — A 3D, browser-based engineering lab simulation for interactive learning. `Three.js` `Express.js` — 🏆 3rd Place, Tech World Hackathon

- **Smart Farm Monitoring System** — An ESP32-based system tracking soil moisture, water level, flame, light, and temperature/humidity, with a WiFi-hosted web dashboard. `ESP32` `C++` `IoT Sensors`

- **Nsupa** — A water-quality monitoring dashboard measuring TDS, turbidity, conductivity, and pH, built for the Global Challenge Lab 2026 to address galamsey-contaminated rivers in Ghana. `ESP32` `C++` `Web Dashboard`

- **K-Map Solver** — A clean, lightweight HTML tool for solving Karnaugh maps quickly. `HTML` `JavaScript`
